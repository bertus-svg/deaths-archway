DEATH'S ARCHWAY

Privacy Policy | ArchTek Nexus

1. Introduction

Welcome to Death's Archway, a game developed and published by ArchTek Nexus.

We respect the privacy of our players and are committed to protecting personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Death's Archway.

This policy applies to the current Android version of Death's Archway and may be updated as the game evolves.

Our privacy commitment: The current version of Death's Archway does not require player accounts and does not intentionally collect or transmit personal information to ArchTek Nexus servers.

2. Information We Collect

The current version of Death's Archway does not provide account registration, online player profiles or developer-operated data collection services.

We do not currently operate analytics, advertising or tracking systems within the game.

The application does not currently request permissions for access to:

  • Precise or approximate location.
  • Camera.
  • Microphone.
  • Contacts.
  • Call logs or SMS messages.

3. Game Data and Local Storage

Game-related information, including progression, game settings, resources and saved game information, may be stored locally on your Android device.

This information is used to provide the gameplay experience and maintain your progress.

ArchTek Nexus does not currently operate a remote game-save server.

4. Device Backup Services

The application currently allows Android system backup functionality.

Depending on your Android device, operating system configuration and backup settings, application data may be included in device backup services operated by your device or platform provider.

Such backup services are governed by the respective provider's privacy policies and settings. ArchTek Nexus does not independently control those services.

5. Third-Party Services

The current version does not intentionally integrate third-party advertising, analytics or player-tracking services.

Android operating system services may provide device-level functionality, including application installation, updates and backup. These services are subject to their respective providers' policies.

6. Internet Connectivity

The current application configuration does not declare the Android INTERNET permission. The current version is designed to operate without developer-operated online accounts or game servers.

Future versions may introduce online functionality. If that happens, this policy will be updated to explain any additional information processing.

7. Children's Privacy

Death's Archway is not currently designed as a service specifically directed at children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the current version of the game.

If future versions introduce online services, additional age-related privacy protections will be considered where applicable.

8. Data Security

Game data is currently stored locally on the player's device. Players should maintain appropriate security on their devices and understand that uninstalling the game or clearing application storage may remove locally stored game progress.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Because the current version does not operate a developer-managed player database, ArchTek Nexus does not currently maintain player account records or remotely stored player profiles.

Players can remove locally stored application data by clearing the application's storage or uninstalling the game.

Any copies retained by device backup services are subject to the relevant platform provider's backup and deletion controls.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Death's Archway develops and introduces new features.

Any significant changes will be reflected on this page. The revision date will be updated accordingly.
