The Experience

Build. Defend. Discover.

Death's Archway combines strategic tower defence with world exploration, construction and resource progression. Your decisions shape how your world develops.

⚒ Construction

Establish your settlements, construct buildings and develop your defensive infrastructure.

◆ Resource Gathering

Discover the materials hidden throughout the different regions and expand your capabilities.

◈ Strategic Defence

Develop defensive positions and prepare your territory against incoming threats.

✦ World Progression

Unlock new regions, advance your settlement and push further into the unknown.
